# Multiple accounts login with Google

Google controls account management when you sign in with Google. To log into the correct account:

- Use **incognito mode** or create separate Chrome/browser profiles for different Google accounts
- Follow [Google's guide](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/13533235?hl=en#zippy=%2Csign-in-with-google) to disassociate Fireworks.ai from a specific Google account
- For complex issues, contact us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/fireworks)
