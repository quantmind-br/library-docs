Yes, Fireworks provides **advance notice before removing models** from serverless:

- **Minimum 2 weeks' notice** before any model removal
- **Longer notice for popular models** — extended timelines based on usage levels

> [!warning]
> Higher-usage models have extended deprecation timelines. Monitor announcements and keep your contact info current.

## Best practices

1. Monitor announcements regularly
2. Prepare a migration plan in advance
3. Test alternative models to ensure continuity
4. Keep your contact information updated for timely notifications
