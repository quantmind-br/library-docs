The Warp referral program rewards users with themes, swag, and gift cards when they invite friends who activate their accounts.

> [!tip]
> Go to **Settings > Referrals** to open the invite dialog.

**Two ways to invite:**
1. Send an invite link directing friends to the product download page.
2. Input their email address to send a referral link directly.

## Your friend gets a theme

If your friend joins and activates Warp after clicking your invite link, they receive a unique theme.

## Referral program terms and conditions

> **Last Updated:** November 27, 2024

### 1 referral: An exclusive theme

The referral theme becomes available in your theme picker after restarting the app. Warp also sends an email alert. This reward is available worldwide.

### All other referral tiers

After hitting other tiers, Warp emails details on claiming rewards. Physical rewards are available to US residents with shipping covered. International users can claim physical rewards but are responsible for customs fees, or choose a digital gift card instead.

### International users

International users choosing physical rewards pay customs fees. See [approximate customs fees by region](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/community/refer-a-friend#approximate-customs-fees-by-region) below.

> [!note]
> For users who qualified before October 15, 2024, Warp covers all shipping and customs costs. After that date, international users are responsible for customs fees.

### Shipping time frame

- US users: 2–3 weeks
- International users: 4–12 weeks

After claiming, receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking link from Printfection. For shipment questions, contact [support@printfection.com](mailto:support@printfection.com) with your order number.

### General terms and conditions

- Referrals must be valid and confirmed by Warp — the referred user must accept the referral via link and log into Warp's desktop app.
- Warp may disqualify referrals deemed fraudulent at its sole discretion.
- Warp reserves the right to modify or terminate the program at any time.

For questions, contact [referrals@warp.dev](mailto:referrals@warp.dev).

## FAQs

**"I've referred people but haven't received an email."**

Allow a few hours for processing. If still nothing, contact [referrals@warp.dev](mailto:referrals@warp.dev).

**"I've claimed my physical reward but have issues receiving my package."**

Respond to the shipping notification email from Printfection, or contact [support@printfection.com](mailto:support@printfection.com) with your order number.

**"I've referred friends, but they didn't use the link. Does it still count?"**

No. Referrals who didn't use your link won't count toward rewards.

**"I've referred friends, but they joined anonymously. Does it still count?"**

No. New users must sign up and actively use Warp. Warp cannot associate anonymous users with referral links.

**"The reward tiers recently changed with new swag items. I previously qualified for X. What happens now?"**

The program was updated November 21, 2024. Warp honors previous reward tiers alongside the updated program until December 15, 2024. Contact [referrals@warp.dev](mailto:referrals@warp.dev) for assistance.
