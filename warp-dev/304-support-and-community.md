## Find your space

### Join the community

The [Warp Slack](https://go.warp.dev/join-slack) is where workflows get shared, bugs get squashed, and ideas turn into features. Talk directly with Warp engineers and other developers.

Built something with Warp? Drop it in `#how-do-you-warp` — the community spotlights projects regularly.

### Build with us on GitHub

Bugs, feature requests, and what's shipping next. Your upvotes decide prioritization.

- [Open an issue](https://github.com/warpdotdev/warp/issues)
- Warp's client is [open source](https://github.com/warpdotdev/warp). See [Contributing to Warp](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/community/contributing) for full contribution paths.

### Attend events

Warp hosts [live events](https://lu.ma/warpdotdev) year-round — product demos, launch celebrations, community Q&As.

### Follow along

- [**X/Twitter**](https://x.com/warpdotdev) — Ship updates and community builds

## Programs

- **Warp Preview** — Try experimental features; feedback directly shapes what ships
- **Refer a Friend** — Earn themes, swag, and gift cards by referring developers
- **Oz Open Source Partnership** — Free Oz agent credits for high-impact open source projects
- **Ambassador program** — For developers leading and growing the Warp community. [Join the Slack](https://go.warp.dev/join-preview) to be notified when applications open.
