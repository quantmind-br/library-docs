## Step 1: Capture and Prepare Your Space

**The first step in any design project is getting a clear view of what you are working with. You need a high quality photo of your room to get the best results from the software. Clear out any clutter that you do not want to see in the final design. Open the curtains to let in as much natural light as possible. Natural light helps the software understand the depth and texture of your walls and floors.**

**Stand in a corner or at the entrance of the room to get the widest angle possible. Try to keep your phone or camera level so the walls do not look tilted. If you have a very large room, take several photos from different angles. This gives you more options when you start the digital makeover. A clean and well-lit photo serves as the perfect canvas for your new concept.**

## Step 2: Choose Your Preferred Interior Style

**Once you have your photo, you need to decide on a direction. Most people have a general idea of what they like but might not know the formal name of the style. You can choose from popular options like minimalist, industrial, or bohemian. Each style has its own set of rules for colors and furniture shapes. Minimalist styles focus on clean lines and very little clutter. Industrial styles use raw materials like brick and metal.**

**The software will take your photo and apply the rules of your chosen style to it. It will suggest new flooring, wall colors, and furniture pieces that match the aesthetic. This is the moment where you can be brave. If you have always wondered if a dark green wall would look good, this is the time to try it. You can switch between styles in seconds to see which one feels most like home.**

## Step 3: Generate Custom Decor and Art

**Standard furniture options are great, but sometimes you want something truly unique. This is where you can use a prompt-based tool to create specific items. You might want a painting that features specific colors to match your new rug. You can describe a sunset over a mountain range in a modern art style. The tool will create a unique image that you can then visualize on your digital wall.**

**This step is also helpful for choosing textiles. You can generate patterns for pillows, curtains, or rugs. If you find a pattern you love, you can use that image to search for similar products in real life. It bridges the gap between a vague idea and a concrete plan. Having these custom visuals makes your room concept feel more personal and less like a showroom.**

## Step 4: Refine the Lighting and Atmosphere

**Lighting changes everything in a room. A space that looks cozy in the evening might look cold and empty during the day. Good design tools allow you to adjust the time of day in your preview. You can see how the sun hits your new sofa at noon. You can also see how your floor lamps create shadows in the evening.**

**Pay attention to where the shadows fall. If a corner looks too dark in the preview, you know you need to add a lamp or a mirror to bounce light around. You can also test different bulb temperatures. Some rooms look better with warm, yellow light, while others need cool, white light to look clean. Adjusting these settings helps you create a realistic plan for your electrical needs.**

## Step 5: Check Scale and Functionality

**The final step in the tutorial is making sure the design actually works for your life. It is easy to get carried away with beautiful images, but you still need to walk through the room. Look at the paths between furniture pieces. Ensure there is enough space to open doors and drawers. The software helps you see if a coffee table is too large for your rug or if a bookshelf blocks the window.**

**Take a moment to look at the room from the perspective of someone sitting down. Does the layout feel inviting? Is the television at a comfortable height? Use the digital preview to move items around until the flow feels natural. Once you are happy with the layout, save your images. You can use these as a shopping list and a guide when you start the physical work.**

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Many people make the mistake of overdecorating. Just because you can add a hundred items to a digital room does not mean you should. A room that is too full feels small and stressful. Keep some space to allow the room to breathe. This is especially important in small apartments or bedrooms.**

**Another common error is ignoring the existing features of the house. Unless you are doing a full renovation, you probably have floors or windows that are staying. Make sure your new design complements these fixed elements. If you have dark wood floors, choose colors that do not clash with them. The goal is to make the new and the old work together in harmony.**

**Do not rely solely on the first result you get. AI tools are very fast, so you should generate several versions of the same room. Sometimes the third or fourth version has a creative idea that you never would have thought of yourself. Compare the different versions side by side to see which parts of each design you like best.**
