## Coming from Something Else?

Warp works well for developers migrating from many other sources (Alacritty, WezTerm, Kitty, Hyper, GNOME Terminal, Konsole, etc.). Drop a note in our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/warpdotdev) so we can prioritize coverage for your tool.
