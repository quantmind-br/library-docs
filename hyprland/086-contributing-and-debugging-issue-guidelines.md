Due to low quality or incomprehensible issues, we prefer to begin bug reports or feature requests as *discussions*, elevating them to issues only if confirmed relevant with enough gathered information.

We are volunteers doing this in our free time. Out of respect, please read this document *fully* before posting an issue or discussion.

## Issue Guidelines

Do **not** open an issue for bugs or missing features. Instead, open a [discussion](https://github.com/hyprwm/Hyprland/discussions).

### Before you start a discussion

Search discussions and issues thoroughly. A discussion for your bug/feature likely already exists — "like" it instead of creating a duplicate.

### Lifecycle of a discussion

A discussion is opened by a user.

**For bugs:**
- Closed if solved, user error, or not a Hyprland issue.
- Stays open if reproducible but cannot be tied to Hyprland clearly.
- Promoted to issue if reproducible and confirmed Hyprland problem (member opens issue linking original discussion).

**For feature requests:**
- Closed if feature is already possible (via scripts, features, or official tools), invalid, or not applicable.
- Stays open if feature is not already possible.
- Promoted to issue if not already possible AND gathers enough attention (member opens issue linking original discussion).

> [!note]
> Issues may stay idle — we are small in number and volunteers.

### Gathering attention for your discussion

Avoid "when fix?" comments. Use the thumbs up reaction instead.

If a discussion reaches 5+ likes and hasn't been promoted, you may ping a member (`@vaxerski`, `@fufexan`, or `@notashelf`).

> [!warning]
> Do **not** ping members for stale discussions. Instead, provide further relevant information or tell interested people to "like" it.
