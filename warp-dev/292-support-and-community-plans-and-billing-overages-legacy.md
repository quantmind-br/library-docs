Usage-based pricing allows continued access to premium AI models after reaching your monthly credit limit (billed at $0.04 per additional credit). Manage in **Settings** > **Billing and usage**.

## Enabling overages

**Team admins** enable or disable "premium model overages" and set a monthly spending limit from the settings dashboard. Individual subscribers manage their own overage settings there.

> [!info]
> Usage-based pricing only applies after reaching your credit limit — no charges until that point, even if overages are enabled.

## How overages work

Overages are managed **at the team level**, even for single-member teams. Each user has their **own credit limit** — only usage **beyond that personal quota** counts as overages, billed collectively at the team level.

**Example:** Plan includes 10,000 credits per team member:

- **User A** hits their 10,000 limit → further usage counts as overages.
- **User B** has used 2,000 → still has 8,000 included credits left.
- User A's overages **do not** consume User B's remaining quota.

Overages are **billed monthly**, or when your team accumulates **$20 worth of charges**, whichever comes first.

## Plan upgrades and cancellations

Upgrading updates your monthly credit limit immediately. Any overages incurred on the lower plan are **still billed** — upgrading does not retroactively remove charges.

Cancelling retains access to premium features until the end of the current billing period. Accrued overages are charged at plan end. See [pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for exact limits.
