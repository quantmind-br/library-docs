## Warp's refund policies

To request a refund, email **billing@warp.dev** with information about your situation.

> [!warning]
> In case of product defect, Warp requires a [Debugging ID](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback#gathering-ai-debugging-id), [Logs](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback#gathering-warp-logs), and "[Help improve Warp](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/privacy-security-and-licensing/privacy#how-to-disable-telemetry-and-crash-reporting)" must be enabled before refunds or credits can be provided.

### Subscription refund policy

**Monthly plans:**
- Full refund if you cancel or downgrade within 24 hours of being charged **and** no credits have been used.
- After 24 hours, or if credits have been used: subscription is cancelled (no future charges), but you retain plan access through the end of the billing cycle.

**Annual plans:**
- Full refund if you cancel or downgrade within 15 days of being charged **and** no credits have been used.
- After 15 days: refund for remaining full months, excluding the first month.

### Add-on Credits | Overages refund policy

- **Add-on Credits**: Refund available only if **none of the credits have been used**. Credits remain available for up to **12 months** from purchase, as long as you have an active subscription. Moving to the Free plan voids remaining Add-on Credits.
- **Overages (Legacy)**: **Non-refundable** — charges are for credits already consumed.
