Fireworks RFT trains frontier models like DeepSeek V3 and Kimi K2 to **outperform closed models for your product use case, using reinforcement learning.**

## Key benefits

- **No infrastructure** — train frontier models without managing GPUs or RL infra
- **Production-ready** — built-in tracing, monitoring, security, and one-click deploy
- **Fast iteration** — from evaluator setup to deployed model in hours, not weeks
