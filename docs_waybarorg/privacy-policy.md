At Waybar, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website, products, or related services. [**Waybar**](https://waybar.org/) is designed to respect user privacy, ensuring no unnecessary data is collected or shared.

Waybar operates as a customizable status bar for Wayland-based systems. Our commitment is to provide open, transparent software that does not compromise your privacy. This document outlines what data we collect, how it’s used, and your rights regarding that data.

### Information We Do Not Collect

Waybar does not collect, track, or store any personal or system data from users.  
The software functions locally on your device and does not transmit data to external servers. We do not collect:

- Personal identifiers such as name, email, or address
- System logs or configurations
- Usage statistics or analytics
- IP addresses or geolocation data

### Information We May Collect (Website Only)

When you visit the official Waybar website, certain limited information may be automatically collected for security and performance purposes, such as:

- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Date and time of access
- Referring and exit pages

This information is used solely to maintain website functionality, ensure security, and improve accessibility. It cannot identify you personally.

### Use of Cookies

The Waybar website may use minimal cookies for essential functionality only.  
These cookies do not track your activity across other websites and do not store personal data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

### Data Storage and Security

All data handled by the Waybar website, if any, is stored securely using industry-standard security measures.  
We do not sell, rent, or share data with any third party. Any technical logs or temporary data are automatically deleted after a short period.

### Third-Party Services

Waybar software and website are designed to minimize third-party dependencies. However, some external components, such as package repositories or analytics services (if used), operate under their own privacy policies. Users are encouraged to review those separately.

### Open-Source Transparency

Waybar is an open-source project. The source code is publicly available for anyone to review, ensuring complete transparency in how the software operates. Users can verify that no personal information is collected or transmitted.

### User Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

- Access and review any data associated with your website interaction (if applicable)
- Request correction or deletion of such data
- Withdraw consent to any data collection activities

To exercise your rights, you may contact the project maintainers through the communication channels listed on the official website.

### Children’s Privacy

Waybar does not target or knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 13.  
If any such information is inadvertently collected, it will be deleted immediately upon discovery.

### Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, regulatory requirements, or changes in our practices. The updated version will always include a revision date for transparency.

### Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Waybar’s data practices, you can reach out to our support or development team directly through the official contact section of our website.

### Commitment to Privacy

Waybar is built with privacy by design. Our philosophy is simple — your system belongs to you. We believe that open-source software should empower users without compromising their privacy or control.
