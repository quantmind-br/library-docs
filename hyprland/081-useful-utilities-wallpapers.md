Launch your wallpaper utility with `exec-once =`.

## hyprpaper

`hyprpaper` is a Hyprland-native wallpaper utility. See [[055-hypr-ecosystem-hyprpaper|hyprpaper]] for usage and config.

## swaybg

Static wallpaper only. Get it [here](https://github.com/swaywm/swaybg).

## wpaperd

Wallpaper daemon with automatic changing and fancy features. [GitHub](https://github.com/danyspin97/wpaperd).

## mpvpaper

mpv wrapper to play video as wallpaper. [GitHub](https://github.com/GhostNaN/mpvpaper).

> [!tip]
> Use [mpvpaper-stop](https://github.com/pvtoari/mpvpaper-stop) to pause background wallpaper and save resources.

## awww

Animated wallpaper daemon with runtime control — restart not needed to change wallpapers. [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/LGFae/awww)

## waypaper

GUI manager for static/animated wallpapers, supports `swaybg` and `swww` backends. [GitHub](https://github.com/anufrievroman/waypaper)

## wallrizz

TUI wallpaper manager with visual selection, supports `swww`, `hyprpaper` and more. [GitHub](https://github.com/5hubham5ingh/WallRizz)
