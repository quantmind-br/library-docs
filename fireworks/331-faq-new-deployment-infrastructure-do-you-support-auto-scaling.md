Yes. Fireworks supports **auto scaling** with:

- **Scale to zero** — conserve resources when idle
- **Configurable scale-up and scale-down velocity** — tune responsiveness
- **Custom scaling rules and thresholds** — match your specific workloads

> [!tip]
> Configure autoscaling on your [[212-api-reference-list-deployments|deployment]] to optimize for cost and latency.

See also: [[025-deployments-autoscaling|Autoscaling deployments]] for detailed configuration.
