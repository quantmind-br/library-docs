Welcome to **Waybar** — A Modern Status Bar for the Wayland World

Waybar was created with a simple yet powerful goal: to bring a fast, flexible, and elegant status bar to the Wayland ecosystem. Designed for developers, power users, and Linux enthusiasts, Waybar empowers you to take full control of your desktop environment with precision, customization, and performance at its core.

Built using modern C++ and GTK, [Waybar](https://waybar.org/) is not just another status bar — it’s a fully modular and themeable framework that adapts to your workflow. Whether you use Sway, Hyprland, or any other Wayland compositor, Waybar seamlessly integrates to give you the tools and visuals you need in a clean and efficient interface.

### Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an open-source, lightweight, and deeply customizable bar that reflects the power and flexibility of Wayland itself. We believe that every user should have the freedom to design their desktop exactly as they want — from minimal displays to feature-rich dashboards. Waybar stands as a testament to the power of simplicity combined with limitless customization.

### What Makes Waybar Unique

- Modular Structure: Every component, from CPU usage to workspace indicators, is a module you can easily control, replace, or enhance.
- Developer-Friendly: With JSON configuration and CSS styling, customization feels natural, transparent, and developer-oriented.
- Performance Driven: Waybar is lightweight and efficient, ensuring that your system stays fast while delivering real-time updates.
- Community Powered: Waybar continues to grow through the dedication of a passionate open-source community contributing new features, modules, and designs.

### Our Vision

We envision Waybar as the definitive status bar for all Wayland users, a tool that remains fast, stable, and endlessly adaptable. Our focus is on improving user experience through continuous innovation, ensuring that Waybar stays compatible with emerging Wayland compositors and modern Linux workflows.

We aim to make customization accessible — not only to advanced users but also to those new to Linux who want a clean, intuitive interface without compromising flexibility.

### Our Values

- Open Source First: We believe in collaboration, transparency, and shared innovation.
- Performance and Stability: Every line of code is optimized to ensure reliability and speed.
- User Freedom: No predefined limits — Waybar gives you total control over your desktop interface.
- Community Collaboration: We grow through feedback, contributions, and ideas from users around the world.

### Join the Waybar Journey

Waybar represents a new era of desktop customization under Wayland — one where speed meets style, and simplicity meets power. Whether you’re a developer seeking fine control or a user crafting a beautiful workspace, Waybar is built to adapt, perform, and inspire.

Our story is defined by our users and contributors — together, we’re building the future of modern Linux desktops.
