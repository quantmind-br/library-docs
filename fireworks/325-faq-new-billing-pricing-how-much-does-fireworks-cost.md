# Fireworks pricing

Fireworks AI operates on a **pay-as-you-go** model for all non-Enterprise usage.

## Pricing breakdown

| Usage type | Billing basis |
|------------|---------------|
| Serverless inference | Per token |
| On-demand deployments | Per GPU usage time |
| Fine-tuning | Per token of training data |

New users automatically receive free credits.

## Enterprise options

For enterprise-grade security and reliability, contact [inquiries@fireworks.ai](mailto:inquiries@fireworks.ai).

> [!info]
> Current pricing details are available on our [Pricing page](https://fireworks.ai/pricing).
