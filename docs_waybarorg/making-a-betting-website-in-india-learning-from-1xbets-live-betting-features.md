## Understanding the Indian Market and Legal Environment

The first thing to consider before introducing a product is to understand the Indian environment thoroughly. The digital economy of the country is booming, and it is anticipated that millions of smartphone users will crave engaging experiences. However, India has a very complicated and strict legal framework for betting activities, which are mainly controlled by the Public Gambling Act of 1867 (the central law) and the freshly formed Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act (PROGA) 2025, which prohibits real-money online gambling at the national level. Hence, if anyone wants to offer such services, the first and foremost step should be to conform to the laws of the land so that they can operate their business without any interruption and fear of the severest penalties, which include both a fine and imprisonment.
