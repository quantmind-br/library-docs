# Pane Dimming & Focus

Improve visual focus in split pane layouts with inactive pane dimming and optional focus-follows-mouse behavior.

## Inactive Pane Dimming

Dims panes that aren't active to indicate which pane is focused.

- **Settings** → **Appearance** → **Panes** → toggle **Dim inactive panes**

> [!info]
> Split panes show a triangle indicator on the top-left corner of the active pane.

## Mouse Focus (Focus Follows Mouse)

The pane under the mouse cursor becomes active automatically.

- **Settings** → **Appearance** → **Panes** → toggle **Focus follows mouse**
