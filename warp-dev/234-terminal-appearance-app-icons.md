> [!info]
> App icons are only available for Warp on macOS. Custom dock icons are not supported.

## How to change the app icon

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Appearance** → **Icon** → **Customize your app icon**
2. Select the desired dock icon from the dropdown menu

![Icon customization dropdown menu](https://docs.warp.dev/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F4009768362-files.gitbook.io%2F~%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FPsjNxoJ0NFCX6rRdHH3%252Fuploads%252Fgit-blob-e6181d548a3118b4335fc5768734c77dcedba26f%252Fcustom-dock-icon-dropdown.png%3Falt%3Dmedia&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=1695d565&sv=2)
