Warp Preview is an early-access program for exploring Warp's newest and most experimental features. It runs independently from the stable build — you can revert to a reliable version at any time.

## Key features

- **Early access** — try experimental features before official release.
- **Faster updates** — frequent patches as new capabilities are refined.
- **Direct impact** — your feedback shapes Warp's future.

See the [launch blog post](https://warp.dev/blog/warp-preview) for more.

## Getting started

1. **Sign in** — [Log in](https://docs.warp.dev/getting-started/installation-and-setup#log-in-to-warp-optional) with your Warp account (required for Warp Preview).
2. **Run side-by-side** — keep Warp Preview alongside your stable Warp installation.

## Important notes

- **Experimental build** — expect occasional bugs or incomplete features.
- **Login required** — needed to gather feedback, diagnose issues, and provide support.
- **Flexibility** — switch between Preview and Stable builds at any time.
- **Distinct icon** — Warp Preview uses a unique icon to distinguish it from the stable version.

## Providing feedback

Share feedback, report bugs, and discuss Preview features in the [community Slack](https://go.warp.dev/join-preview). You can also [send feedback directly from the app](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback#sending-warp-feedback).
