# LinkedIn authentication email

Fireworks uses the **primary email address** from your LinkedIn account as your account identifier.

## Changing your primary email

1. Go to [LinkedIn email settings](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/manage-email-addresses)
2. Click **Add email address** or select an existing one to make primary

## Switching between Fireworks accounts

Update your LinkedIn primary email before logging in to switch which Fireworks account LinkedIn authenticates into. This lets you:

- Log into different Fireworks accounts using the same LinkedIn account
- Switch between personal and work accounts
- Maintain separate billing and usage tracking per email address

> [!info]
> The authentication uses whichever email is set as primary at login time.
