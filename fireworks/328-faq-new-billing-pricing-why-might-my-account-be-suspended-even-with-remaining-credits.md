Your account may be suspended due to:

### Monthly usage limits

Each tier includes a **monthly usage limit**, independent of any credits. Once you hit this limit, your service is suspended — even with credits remaining. **Usage limits reset at the start of each month.**

### Pre-purchased credits do not cap charges

Credits do not prevent additional charges. You can exceed your credits and be billed for the overage.

> [!example]
> Have `$20` in credits but incur `$83` in usage → billed `$63` at month-end.

If you're suspended and need assistance, contact `inquiries@fireworks.ai`.
