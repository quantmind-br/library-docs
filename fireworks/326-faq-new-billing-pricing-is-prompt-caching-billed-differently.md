Cached prompt tokens are **discounted compared to uncached tokens** for serverless models.

- **Default discount: 50%**
- Exact discount **varies by model** — check the [[072-guides-prompt-caching|Prompt Caching guide]] for model-specific pricing

> [!info]
> Check the [Model Library](https://fireworks.ai/models) for cached vs uncached input token pricing per model.
