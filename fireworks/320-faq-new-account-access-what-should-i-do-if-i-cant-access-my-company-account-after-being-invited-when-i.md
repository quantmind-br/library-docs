# Can't access company account after invitation

This occurs when you have multiple accounts associated with the same email address (e.g., personal Google account + invited company account).

## Resolution steps

1. Email [inquiries@fireworks.ai](mailto:inquiries@fireworks.ai) from the email address associated with both accounts
2. Include in your email:
   - Your personal account ID (e.g., `username-44ace8`)
   - The company account ID you need access to (e.g., `company-a57b2a`)
   - Mention you're having trouble accessing your company account

> [!note]
> Support can resolve this once they verify your email ownership. This is a known scenario.
