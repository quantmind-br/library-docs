Preconfigured dotfile collections provide a DE-like Hyprland experience without manual setup.

## ML4W

[My Linux 4 Work](https://www.ml4w.com/) by Stephan Raabe — GUI settings apps, pre-configured panels, welcome app.

- [GitHub](https://github.com/mylinuxforwork/dotfiles)
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@mylinuxforwork)

![ML4W screenshot](https://i.ibb.co/7rxTRrw/395854121-47ed1ae0-a660-46f3-9bf5-917da0d3f675.png)

## JaKooLit

Feature-rich, well-maintained dotfiles with installation scripts for many distros and theme options.

- [GitHub](https://github.com/JaKooLit/Hyprland-Dots)
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Ja.KooLit)

![JaKooLit screenshot](https://i.ibb.co/7tMsnTv/default-waybar.png)

## end\_4

Material styling with many apps. Requires minor tinkering.

- [GitHub](https://github.com/end-4/dots-hyprland)

![end-4 screenshot](https://github.com/end-4/dots-hyprland/assets/97237370/5e081770-0f1e-45c4-ad9c-3d19f488cd85)

## HyDE

Minimal, clean, terminal-focused dotfiles by the HyDE Project.

- [GitHub](https://github.com/HyDE-Project/HyDE)

![HyDE screenshot](https://i.ibb.co/W3SYJCc/showcase-2-2412602747.png)

## Omarchy

Opinionated Arch + Hyprland setup by DHH.

- [Website](https://omarchy.org/)

![Omarchy screenshot](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/Cft6mZDzIng/maxresdefault.jpg)
