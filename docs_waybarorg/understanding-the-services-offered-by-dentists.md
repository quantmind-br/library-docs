Healthy teeth, gums, and jaw support proper bite function, speech, and nutrition. Prioritizing your dental health also reduces the risk of infections such as periodontitis, which spreads systemically. Evaluating the services a dentist offers allows you to receive targeted care for your specific needs, and this can lead to better outcomes. Here are the services provided by professional dentists:

## **Orthodontic Dentistry Services**

Orthodontic services, such as braces, help address misalignment of the teeth, so the bite and jaw can function properly. Braces work by applying consistent pressure, which then causes the teeth to gradually shift into their proper, functional positions. These orthodontic appliances come as clear aligners and traditional metal braces. Traditional metal braces use brackets bonded to teeth and connected by wires. The wires tighten over time to help reposition the teeth, and this makes them more suitable for complex misalignments.

Clear aligners consist of nearly invisible plastic trays that can be removed when you’re brushing or eating. These plastic trays snap over teeth, which allows for gradual straightening. Traditional metal braces offer precise control and durability, while clear aligners are easier to maintain, discreet, and convenient. ## **Oral Surgery Services**

Dental specialists provide oral surgery to help address defects or abnormalities such as cleft lip and palate, impacted teeth, and jaw misalignment. You need oral surgery if you experience issues like persistent jaw pain or difficulty opening the mouth. Oral surgical procedures can also help address injuries or diseases such as broken jaws, facial bone fractures, and oral cysts and tumors.

Early oral surgery removes severely damaged teeth; this prevents infections from spreading, helping protect surrounding gums and bone in the long term. Professional dental specialists combine safe anesthesia options and advanced technology, such as 3D imaging, to promote accurate procedures, patient comfort, and smooth recovery. Some forms of oral surgery procedures that a professional dentist provides include:

- Corrective jaw surgery
- Tooth extraction
- Osseous tissue surgery
- Bone grafting

## **General Dentistry Services**

General dentistry focuses on preventing, diagnosing, and offering treatment tailored to families’ specific oral health needs. It includes routine check-ups, enabling dentists to easily detect issues such as early-stage cavities or gingivitis before they worsen. This service also includes restoring damaged teeth with fillings or crowns, and educating patients on proper oral habits. Some general dentistry services a specialist may recommend include fluoride treatments and cavity repair.

## **Restorative Dentistry Services**

A dentist surgically places dental implants into the jawbone to help replace missing tooth roots. These titanium posts serve as stable anchors for artificial teeth and also help support adjacent teeth. They look and feel like natural teeth while staying secure for comfortable chewing. Signs you need dental implants include missing teeth, pain when chewing, and even loose dentures.
