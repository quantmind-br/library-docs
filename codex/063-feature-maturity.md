# Feature Maturity

Labels indicate reliability, expected changes, and support level.

| Maturity | Meaning | Guidance |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Under development | Not ready for use | Don't use |
| Experimental | Unstable; may be removed or changed | Use at your own risk |
| Beta | Ready for broad testing; some aspects may change based on feedback | OK for evaluation and pilots; expect small changes |
| Stable | Fully supported, documented, consistent over time | Safe for production; removals typically go through deprecation |
